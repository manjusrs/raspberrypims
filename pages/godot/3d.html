Step into 3D

GEM WORLD
Name:  

What you're doing

Four gems. Three are easy. One is too high. Your job is to reach them.

1Run it

Double-click world_3d.tscn. Press F6 (Run Current Scene). On a Mac laptop, try Fn + F6.

F5 always runs Star Catcher. F6 runs the open scene.

Arrow keys move. Space jumps.

2Moon jump

Open player_3d.gd. Try these values:

SPEED = 6.0
JUMP_POWER = 9.0   # try 14
GRAVITY = 20.0     # try 4

3Build a staircase

Set JUMP_POWER back to 9.0.

Click Platform2 in the Scene list. Press Ctrl + D to duplicate (on Mac, Cmd + D). Drag the arrows to place it.

red = x left and right green = y up and down blue = z forward and back

4Make it yours

PLAYER_COLOR in player_3d.gd

SKY_COLOR and WIN_MESSAGE in world_3d.gd

GEM_COLOR in gem_3d.gd

Drag gem_3d.tscn into the world for more gems.

Lost in space

Click Player and press F to fly back.

Scroll to zoom.

Falling off the world resets you to the start.
