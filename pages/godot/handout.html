What you're doing

You start with a finished game. Your job is to make it yours.

Golden rule: Predict. Change one thing. Play. If it breaks, press Ctrl + Z (on Mac, Cmd + Z).

0Open the project

Download star-catcher.zip.

Unzip it. Windows: right-click, Extract All. Mac: double-click.

Do not skip the unzip!

Open Godot, click Import, pick project.godot, then click Import & Edit.

Press F5 to play (on a Mac laptop, try Fn + F5). Press Space to start. Arrow keys move. Press F8 to stop.

Your map of the editor

In player.gd, change SPEED (default 300) and PLAYER_SIZE (default 1.0).

5Mission 5 · Write your own code, the dash

In player.gd, find the line # MISSION 5 and type the two lines below under it.

	if Input.is_key_pressed(KEY_SHIFT):
		speed = SPEED * 2

An if asks a yes or no question. One Tab before if, two Tabs before speed. Do not forget the colon.

Red errors are normal. Check the line you just typed, and make sure the Tabs are real Tabs, not spaces.
