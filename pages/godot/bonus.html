Name:  

A · Bonus time

In main.gd, find func star_caught. After score += points, add the amber line.

func star_caught(points):
	score += points
	time_left += 2

B · Ouch

In main.gd, find func player_hit. After lives -= 1, add the amber line.

func player_hit():
	lives -= 1
	score -= 1

C · Grow when you eat

In func star_caught, add the amber line.

func star_caught(points):
	score += points
	$Player.scale *= 1.1

D · Enemies get angry

In enemy.gd, find func _process. Add the amber lines.

func _process(delta):
	var extra = get_tree().current_scene.score * 10
	position += direction * (SPEED + extra) * delta

E · Swap the art

Drag a PNG into the art folder.

Open player.tscn, click Sprite2D, and drag the picture onto Texture in the Inspector.

Set PLAYER_COLOR to Color.WHITE.
