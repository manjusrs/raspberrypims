Godot Game Lab · Middle School

Remix a game in 3 hours

Overview

You get a finished game called Star Catcher. Your job is to make it yours. Change the colors, the speed, and the rules. Write your first lines of code. Then step into a 3D world. No coding experience needed.

What you will do

WhenWhat happens
First 25 minutesMeet Godot. Open the game and play it.
Next 35 minutesMissions 1 to 3. Sign it, paint it, and tune your hero.
Break.
Next 50 minutesMissions 4 and 5. Change the rules and write your own code.
Break.
Next 25 minutesStep into 3D with Gem World.
Last 25 minutesFree remix and showcase.

What you will learn

  • How a game is made of scenes and scripts
  • What a variable is
  • How an if statement makes a choice
  • How 3D adds a third direction

Getting Started

1Get Godot

Download Godot 4.5 or newer from godotengine.org/download. Pick the standard version, not .NET. It is free.

On workshop laptops, Godot is already installed.

2Download the project

Download star-catcher.zip.

3Unzip it

Windows: right-click, Extract All. Mac: double-click.

Do not skip the unzip!

4Import in Godot

Open Godot, click Import, pick project.godot inside the star-catcher folder, then click Import & Edit.

5Play

Press Play (F5). On a Mac laptop you may need Fn + F5. Press Space to start. Move with the arrow keys.

Stuck?

Nothing happens when I press Play: click the game window first.

I see red text: press Ctrl + Z (on Mac, Cmd + Z).

I cannot find the folder: check Downloads.
